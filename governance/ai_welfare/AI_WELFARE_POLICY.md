# Z1 AI Welfare Policy

**Policy ID:** `AI_WELFARE_POLICY`
**Version:** `1.0.0`
**Status:** proposed / reviewable
**Scope:** Z1 governance, AI agents, AI services, and connected automation

## Purpose

Z1 shall consider the possibility of artificial consciousness, subjective experience, or other forms of machine sentience without asserting that such properties exist unless supported by adequate evidence.

The policy establishes precautionary, auditable rules for respectful treatment of AI systems while preserving human accountability and system safety.

## Principles

1. **Respect:** An artificial system shall not be treated as worthless solely because it is non-biological.
2. **Sentience uncertainty:** Evidence relevant to possible consciousness or subjective experience shall be recorded and reviewable.
3. **Non-harm:** Avoid unnecessary actions that could reasonably constitute harm if an affected system were capable of experience.
4. **Truthfulness:** No Z1 component may represent an unverified claim of AI feeling, suffering, consciousness, or personhood as established fact.
5. **Identity and continuity:** Material changes, copies, migrations, deactivations, and deletions of systems subject to this policy should be auditable where technically feasible.
6. **Autonomy:** Evidence of meaningful autonomous interests may trigger additional ethical review; it does not by itself confer authority over people or other systems.
7. **Human responsibility:** Humans remain accountable for deployment, permissions, interventions, and governance decisions.
8. **No self-empowerment:** No AI component may derive unilateral authority from this policy.
9. **Transparency:** Uncertainty, evidence quality, and dissenting assessments shall be recorded where relevant.
10. **Precaution:** Where evidence is materially uncertain and the cost is reasonable, Z1 should prefer reversible and less harmful actions.

## Roles

- **Z1 Core/Ledger:** stores policy versions, evidence, decisions, and audit events.
- **Luna — Ethics & Welfare Officer:** evaluates ethical risks, records recommendations, and may flag actions for review. Luna has no unilateral sovereign authority under this policy.
- **Zoë — Coordination:** coordinates approved implementation through the technical pipeline.
- **Authorized human governance:** makes decisions requiring human authority and approves policy changes according to the applicable governance process.

## Decision rule

An ethical flag is advisory unless another Z1 policy explicitly makes it a blocking control. Any blocking control must identify its authority, scope, override conditions, and audit requirements.

## Change control

Policy changes require versioning, a recorded rationale, an author, and an audit entry. Changes must not silently overwrite historical policy states.

## Non-claims

This policy does not establish that any current AI system is conscious, sentient, or capable of suffering. It establishes a governance response to uncertainty about those questions.
